Privacy Policy

Muhakah Publisher is a private content publishing tool operated for the Muhakah channel. This policy explains what data the tool processes and how it is protected.

Effective date: 7 November 2026

1. Who we are

Muhakah Publisher ("the tool", "we", "us") is an independent content publishing tool operated by the Muhakah channel. It is used to prepare and publish Muhakah's own videos to authorized TikTok accounts through TikTok's official APIs.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Muhakah Publisher application, its authorization flow and the content it uploads. It does not apply to TikTok's own services, which are governed by TikTok's privacy policy.

3. Information we process

  • Account information: TikTok account identifiers, display name, avatar and the permissions you grant.
  • Authentication data: OAuth access and refresh tokens used to publish to your authorized account.
  • Content: video files, captions, thumbnails and related metadata that you choose to publish.
  • Technical data: upload status, timestamps, error messages and operational logs.

4. How we use it

  • Authenticate with TikTok and publish the content you authorize.
  • Show upload status and troubleshoot failures.
  • Maintain security, prevent duplicate uploads and comply with platform rules.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train third-party models.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with:

  • TikTok, through its official APIs, to publish content and report status.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the tool and encrypted storage, under access controls.
  • Authorities where required by applicable law.

6. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted. Access is restricted to the authorized operator. We use access controls, least-privilege permissions and routine credential review. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the data.

7. Retention

  • OAuth tokens are kept until you revoke access or they expire.
  • Uploaded media is kept until you delete it or request deletion.
  • Operational logs are kept for up to 90 days unless needed to investigate a security or publishing issue.

8. Your choices

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in your TikTok account settings. You can request access, correction or deletion of data by contacting us through the link below. Revoking access stops future API publishing.

9. Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required by TikTok in their region.

10. International processing

TikTok and our infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their own policies and safeguards apply to their processing.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the tool or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: open an issue in the Muhakah Legal repository.
