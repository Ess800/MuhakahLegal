Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of data associated with Muhakah Publisher at any time.

How to request deletion

Open an issue at github.com/Ess800/MuhakahLegal/issues with the subject “Data deletion request” and include the TikTok, Facebook or Instagram account identifier you want removed.

What will be deleted

  • Stored OAuth tokens for that account
  • Publishing records and upload status logs associated with that account
  • Any cached media or metadata held by the tool

What cannot be deleted by the tool

Content already published to TikTok, Facebook or Instagram must be deleted through that platform. Meta and TikTok retain their own records under their policies.

Timing

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days after verifying the request. We may retain records required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.
